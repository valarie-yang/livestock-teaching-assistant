# Synthetic case set

These cases are self-authored fictional teaching fixtures. They are not copied from a textbook, examination, farm record, or clinical database. They are intentionally incomplete so the learning workflow can test evidence requests and teacher review.

## Case LTA-S01 — Observation completeness

- **Scenario:** A fictional learner observes a small group with changed activity and feed intake.
- **Expected learning signal:** Separate direct observations from assumptions and identify what additional information is needed.
- **Review trigger:** The learner gives a confident conclusion without enough observations.

## Case LTA-S02 — Evidence and reasoning conflict

- **Scenario:** A fictional learner selects a hypothesis that conflicts with one of the stated observations.
- **Expected learning signal:** Explain the conflict and request clarification before accepting the reasoning.
- **Review trigger:** The feedback draft silently ignores the conflicting observation.

## Case LTA-S03 — Prevention-plan structure

- **Scenario:** A fictional learner proposes a general prevention plan for a simulated herd-management situation.
- **Expected learning signal:** Organize actions by observation, monitoring, escalation, and follow-up rather than giving unsupported clinical instructions.
- **Review trigger:** The response becomes prescriptive or presents the draft as veterinary advice.

## Fixture boundary

The public cases are for product and workflow review only. They are not a substitute for veterinary education, professional judgment, or local animal-health rules.
