# Product brief

## Users

- Learners in livestock, animal health, or veterinary-support courses;
- teachers who review reasoning and assign remediation;
- content reviewers who approve synthetic learning cases.

## Job to be done

When a learner encounters a livestock health scenario, help them express an observation-based hypothesis and prevention plan, then give the teacher enough structured evidence to review the reasoning and decide what happens next.

## MVP scope

1. Select a fictional case.
2. Record observations and learner reasoning.
3. Score against a transparent rubric.
4. Draft structured feedback.
5. Route low-confidence or disputed results to teacher review.
6. Assign a follow-up exercise and record progress.

## Out of scope

- clinical diagnosis or treatment prescription;
- autonomous content publication;
- use of real student, farm, school, or animal records;
- unlicensed textbook or exam-content reproduction;
- claims of formal school deployment or measured learning outcomes.

## Product decisions

- Keep observation, reasoning, feedback, and teacher decision as separate objects.
- Prefer transparent criteria over opaque single scores.
- Treat uncertainty as a review state, not as a confident answer.
- Make remediation part of the workflow rather than an afterthought.
- Use synthetic content in the public package so the product design can be reviewed without exposing protected materials.
