# Public data, privacy, and content boundary

## May appear here

- Self-authored fictional case cards;
- generic learning and review workflows;
- abstract rubric and state models;
- synthetic screenshots or static examples;
- product decisions and non-sensitive evaluation definitions.

## Must remain private

- textbook text, images, page references, or licensed media;
- recovered examination questions, answer explanations, or commercial question banks;
- student, class, teacher, expert, school, farm, or animal records;
- platform URLs, project IDs, credentials, databases, logs, signed records, and release bundles;
- provider keys, internal prompts, private deployment details, and trial outputs.

## Claim boundary

This package demonstrates product design and a safe public reconstruction. It does not claim formal school rollout, clinical validity, production model quality, or ownership of the private source project's implementation or content.
